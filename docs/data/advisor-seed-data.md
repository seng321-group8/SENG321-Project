# Advisor Seed Data

## Purpose
The system needs advisor data (interests, grades served, office hours) for development, testing and demonstration.

## How the data was prepared
- The course instructor advised the team not to collect the data from each advisor in person, because the advisors do not have time.
- The team shared 24 advisors between the six team members (four advisors each) and filled in the advisor form on their behalf.
- Interest keywords were taken from public sources: the advisors' faculty web pages and their publications on Google Scholar.
- Office hours, grades served and the online preference were generated randomly.

## Generation rules
- Office hours: 2 to 4 slots a week, 30 minutes each.
- Grades served: between 1 and 4 grades per advisor.
- Online preference: set per advisor.
- Interests: 1 to 5 per advisor, from the shared interest list.

## Status of the data
This is seed data. It is not a response from the advisors. It is used only for development and demonstration.

## Privacy
Advisor names and e-mail addresses are not stored in this repository. Advisors are identified by codes (ADV-01 to ADV-24).
